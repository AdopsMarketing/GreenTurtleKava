<h2><b>What is Kava?</b></h2>
<p><span>Kava is a drink made from the roots of the Piper methysticum plant, which is native to the South Pacific islands. The roots are ground into a powder, mixed with water, and strained to create a milky, earthy beverage.</span></p>
