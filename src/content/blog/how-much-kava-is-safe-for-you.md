<h2>Understanding Kava and Its Effects</h2>
<p>Kava is an herbal concoction prepared from the root of the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/biochemistry-genetics-and-molecular-biology/piper-methysticum#:~:text=Kava%20Kava%20(Piper%20methysticum)%20is,Pittler%20and%20Ernst%2C%202000).">Piper methysticum plant from the Pacific region</a>. The root is crushed to a powder and mixed with water to make the drink which allows people to relax, reduces tension, and facilitates companionship.</p><p>Flavonoid-like substances in Kava are credited for the drug’s ability to induce relaxation and sedation. These compounds work on the brain receptors to cause a feeling of relaxation and mental focus that is not accompanied by any decrease in cognition.   </p><p>This <a href="https://greenturtlekava.co/health-benefits-of-kava-why-you-should-try-it/">unique effect of kava</a> makes it a preferable drink for those who want to relax, but do not want to do it at the cost of their health and consciousness.</p>
