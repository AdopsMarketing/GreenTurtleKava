<p>Featuring kavalactones as their active compounds, Kava is known to affect the brain receptors. It induces a feeling of calmness and relaxation. If you are experiencing <a href="https://greenturtlekava.co/how-does-kava-help-in-modern-anxiety-management/">anxiety</a>, you might want to head to the nearest kava bar. <h2>The Risks Associated With Kava</h2>
<p>Those who are on norepinephrine and dopamine reuptake inhibitors (NDRIs) for depression should not consume kava without a doctor’s consultation. Likewise, you can’t mix kava with alcohol, nor should you have these two together. </p><p>Since both work in pretty much a similar way, they may heighten your risk of getting liver toxicity or cognitive issues, as Kava can intensify the effect of alcohol. Kava must also be avoided if you are taking anxiety medication or are diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease. It might interfere with these meds.</p><p>A few common side effects of Kava include drowsiness, nausea, loss of appetite, indigestion, low sex drive, red eyes, and headaches. People with liver disease, depression, and kidney diseases must avoid Kava or consult their healthcare professional before having it.</p>
