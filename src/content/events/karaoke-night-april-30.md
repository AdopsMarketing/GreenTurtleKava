# Green Turtle Kava Bar

## Karaoke Night

Get ready to sing your heart out at Green Turtle Kava Bar’s Karaoke Night! Join us on Wednesday, April 30th at 8 PM for an evening full of music, laughter, and good vibes. Whether you’re a seasoned performer or just want to have some fun with friends, everyone’s welcome to take the mic. Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere with your favorite kava drinks while cheering on all the performers. It’s all about great songs, great company, and unforgettable memories.
