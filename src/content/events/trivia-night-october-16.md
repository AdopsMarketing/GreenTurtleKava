# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Trivia Night

Green Turtle Kava Bar’s trivia night gives participants the chance to win free tabs for the winning teams, all while enjoying refreshing kava drinks. Teams can compete in a fun, friendly environment, testing their knowledge across various topics. Whether you’re a trivia enthusiast or just looking for a lively evening out with friends, the mix of competition and delicious kava makes for an enjoyable night.
