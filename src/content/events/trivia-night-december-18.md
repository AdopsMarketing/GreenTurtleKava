# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Trivia Night

Join the fun at Green Turtle Kava Bar’s Trivia Night! Test your knowledge on a variety of exciting topics, team up with friends, and enjoy a lively evening filled with friendly competition. Sip on refreshing kava drinks as you work your way to the top for a chance to win free bar tabs awarded to the best teams. Whether you’re a trivia enthusiast or just looking for a fun night out, this event offers something for everyone.
