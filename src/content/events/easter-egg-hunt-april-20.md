# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Easter Egg Hunt

The Green Turtle Kava Bar in St. Augustine is hosting a fun-filled **Easter Egg Hunt** on **Sunday, April 20th at 8 PM**. The event invites guests to join in the festive spirit by searching for hidden Easter eggs throughout the venue. Prizes hidden inside the eggs range from **free drinks** to **sweet candy treats**, adding a playful twist to your evening. Whether you’re in it for the goodies or just to enjoy the laid-back atmosphere, it’s a great way to celebrate the holiday with friends and family.
