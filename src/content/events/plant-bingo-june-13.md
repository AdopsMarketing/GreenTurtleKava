# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Plant Bingo

Calling all plant lovers! Join us at Green Turtle Kava Bar – St. Augustine for a fun-filled evening of Plant Bingo, where you can test your plant knowledge, connect with fellow plant enthusiasts, and even win plants along the way. Whether you’re a seasoned plant parent or just starting your collection, this event is a great opportunity to learn new plant facts, make new friends, and enjoy a relaxed community atmosphere. Stop by on **June 13, 2026, at 8 PM** for an evening of plants, prizes, and good vibes!
