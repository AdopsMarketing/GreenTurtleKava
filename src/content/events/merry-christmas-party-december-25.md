# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Merry Christmas Party

Celebrate the magic of Christmas at **Green Turtle Kava** on **December 25th** with great food, music, and festive vibes. Enjoy dinner starting at 6 PM, then dance the night away with a live DJ. Show off your holiday spirit by dressing as Santa, Mrs. Claus, or an elf and enter our Christmas outfit contest for a chance to win a free bar tab. Bring your friends and join us for a fun-filled Christmas celebration you won’t want to miss
