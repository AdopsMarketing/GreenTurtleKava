# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

The Green Turtle Kava Bar hosts various events, including open mic nights, where you can enjoy live performances in a relaxed atmosphere. Participants are encouraged to share their talents, whether it be music, poetry, or other forms of creative expression.
