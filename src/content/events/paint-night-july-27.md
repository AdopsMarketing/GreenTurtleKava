# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Paint Night

Unleash your creativity at Paint Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar! Join us for a fun and relaxing group painting session where you can enjoy kava drinks, express yourself artistically, and connect with fellow art enthusiasts. Whether you’re a seasoned painter or a beginner, this guided event is perfect for all skill levels. Don’t miss out on a colorful evening of artistic expression and good vibes.
