## Green Turtle Kava Bar at ST. Augustine  
Trivia Night

Test your knowledge at Trivia Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar! Enjoy a night of brain-teasing questions, friendly competition, and free bar tabs for winners. Gather your team, sip on refreshing kava, and take on the challenge. Whether you’re a trivia pro or just in it for fun, it’s all about great vibes and good times.
