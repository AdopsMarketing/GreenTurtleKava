# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St. Augustine

## Open Mic Night

Step up to the mic at Open Mic Night! All acts are welcome—music, poetry, comedy, or any talent you want to share. Join a supportive crowd, enjoy kava, and showcase your creativity. Whether you’re performing or just watching, it’s a night full of talent and great vibes!
