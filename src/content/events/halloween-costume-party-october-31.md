# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Halloween Costume Party

Join Green Turtle Kava Bar for a thrilling Halloween Costume Party! Come dressed in your best costume and compete for a chance to win free tabs for the night. It’s the perfect way to celebrate Halloween with friends, family, great music, and spooky vibes. Enjoy the kava drinks, show off your creative costume, and be part of an unforgettable evening.
