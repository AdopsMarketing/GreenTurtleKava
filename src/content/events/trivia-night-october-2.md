# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Trivia Night

At Green Turtle Kava Bar’s trivia night, winners can earn free tabs for their team as part of the event’s rewards. It’s a fun, interactive way to test your knowledge while competing with friends and family. Enjoy the friendly atmosphere and work together to claim the top spot.
