# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

All acts are welcome at Green Turtle Kava Bar’s open mic night, offering a platform for performers of any kind. Singers, poets, and comedians can all take the stage and connect with a friendly audience, making it a great night for both participants and attendees.
