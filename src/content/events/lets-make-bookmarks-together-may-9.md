# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Let’s Make Bookmarks Together

Join us at Green Turtle Kava Bar – St. Augustine for a fun and creative afternoon making handmade bookmarks together! Whether you love reading, crafting, or just want a relaxing way to spend your Saturday, this event is the perfect excuse to unwind, sip your favorite kava, and create something special. Plus, your custom bookmark makes a thoughtful Mother’s Day gift for the following day. Come hang out, get creative, and enjoy the chill vibes with the Turtle Tribe on Saturday, May 9th at 2 PM!
