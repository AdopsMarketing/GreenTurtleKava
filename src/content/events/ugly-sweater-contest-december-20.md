# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Ugly Sweater Contest

Celebrate the season at Green Turtle Kava Bar with our Ugly Sweater Contest night. Dress up in your most outrageous holiday sweater and enjoy a laid-back evening of kava, community, and laughs. Show off your festive style, soak in the cozy vibes, and enjoy the friendly competition. Whether you’re here to win or just to unwind, it’s a night made for holiday fun.
