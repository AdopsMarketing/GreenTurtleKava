# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St. Augustine

## Karaoke Night

Join Karaoke Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar for an evening of music, laughter, and good vibes! Take the stage solo or sing with friends and show off your favorite tunes. Whether you’re a seasoned performer or just having fun, everyone is welcome to join in. Sip on kava, enjoy the lively atmosphere, and cheer on fellow singers. Don’t miss out on a night of unforgettable moments!
