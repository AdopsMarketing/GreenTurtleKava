# Green Turtle Kava Bar

## Open Mic Night

Get ready for a fun and creative evening at **Green Turtle Kava Bar** on **October 6th at 7 PM** for **Open Mic Night**! Share your talent or simply sit back and enjoy live music, poetry, comedy, and more, **all acts are welcome**. Sip on relaxing kava or herbal teas as you soak in the good vibes and supportive atmosphere. It’s the perfect night to unwind, connect, and celebrate creativity with the community.
