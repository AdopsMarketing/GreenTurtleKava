# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

Unleash your creativity at Open Mic Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar! All acts are welcome—musicians, poets, comedians, and more. Enjoy kava drinks and a relaxed atmosphere while showcasing your talent or cheering on others. It’s a night of connection, creativity, and good vibes.
