# Green Turtle Kava Bar

## Open Mic Night

Come share your talent at Open Mic Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar! Whether you’re a musician, poet, comedian, or just want to try something new, all acts are welcome on our stage. It’s a relaxed, judgment-free space where creativity and community come together. Grab a kava, take a seat, or step up to the mic and be part of a night full of authentic expression and good vibes.
