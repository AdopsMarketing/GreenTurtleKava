# Green Turtle Kava Bar at ST. Augustine  
Open Mic Night

Step up to the mic at The Green Turtle Kava Bar and let your voice be heard! Whether you’re a poet, comedian, musician, or any other performer, all acts are welcome at Open Mic Night. Share your talent, connect with a lively crowd, and enjoy an evening of creativity, kava, and great vibes. No matter your skill level, the spotlight is yours—come join the fun!
