# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Physical Therapy Night

Physical Therapy Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar was a unique blend of wellness and community vibes. The relaxed atmosphere made it the perfect setting to learn about healing through movement. Attendees enjoyed hands-on sessions, expert advice on injury prevention, and the calming effects of kava. It was a refreshing evening that combined education, connection, and a chill environment.
