# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

Green Turtle Kava Bar hosts an Open Mic Night, inviting all acts to perform in a relaxed, supportive setting. Whether you’re a musician, comedian, poet, or storyteller, this event offers a welcoming stage for everyone to showcase their talent. Grab a kava drink, take the mic, and enjoy an evening filled with creativity and community
