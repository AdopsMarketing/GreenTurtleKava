# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Trivia Night

Compete, laugh, and unwind at Green Turtle Kava Bar’s Trivia Night! Challenge your knowledge with friends, enjoy delicious kava, and aim to win prizes like free bar tabs. Whether you’re a trivia buff or just looking for a great night out, this event offers a fun atmosphere with something for everyone.
