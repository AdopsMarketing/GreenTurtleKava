# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

Step into the spotlight at Green Turtle Kava Bar’s Open Mic Night! All acts are welcome—bring your music, poetry, comedy, or any creative performance. Enjoy kava drinks, a welcoming audience, and a relaxed atmosphere perfect for showcasing your talents or cheering others on. It’s a fun, supportive evening for performers and spectators alike!
