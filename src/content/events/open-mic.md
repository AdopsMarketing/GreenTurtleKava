# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic

Join us at Green Turtle Kava Bar for our Open Mic Nights! Showcase your talent, whether it’s music, poetry, or comedy, in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere. Perfect for performers of all levels, come share your passion or enjoy the creativity of others. Sip on kava while experiencing a night of live entertainment and community vibes.
