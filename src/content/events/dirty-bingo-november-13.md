## Dirty Bingo

Join Green Turtle Kava Bar for a fun and edgy twist on a classic game with Dirty Bingo! Expect laughs, cheeky prizes, and a lively atmosphere as you play rounds of bingo with a playful, adult twist. Bring friends or make new ones while enjoying kava drinks and a night of unconventional entertainment.
