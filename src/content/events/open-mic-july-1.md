# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Open Mic All Acts Welcome

Showcase your talent at our lively open mic night at the kava bar! Enjoy an evening of music, poetry, and storytelling in a welcoming atmosphere. Sip on delicious kava drinks while local artists take the stage to share their creativity. Whether you’re a performer or a spectator, come experience a night of community and entertainment. Join us for an unforgettable open mic event!
