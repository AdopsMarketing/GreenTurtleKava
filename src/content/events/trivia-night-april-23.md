# Green Turtle Kava Bar

## Trivia Night

Join us at Green Turtle Kava Bar for an exciting Trivia Night where brains meet brews, kava, and good vibes flow! Bring your friends, show off your smarts, and compete in a fun, laid-back atmosphere. Whether you’re a trivia pro or just in it for the laughs, there’s something for everyone. Best part? Winners score free bar tabs! It’s the perfect midweek boost of fun, friendly competition, great company, and your favorite kava drinks.
