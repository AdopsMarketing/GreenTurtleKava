# Green Turtle Kava Bar at St Augustine

## Trivia Night

Bring your friends to Green Turtle Kava Bar’s Trivia Night for an evening of fun and competition! Test your knowledge, sip on delicious kava drinks, and compete for free tabs awarded to the top teams. Whether you’re a trivia enthusiast or just looking for a good time, this event is perfect for everyone.
