<h2>Open Mic Night</h2>
<p>Come share your talent at Open Mic Night at Green Turtle Kava Bar! Whether you’re a musician, poet, comedian, or just want to try something new, all acts are welcome on our stage. Grab a kava, take a seat, or step up to the mic and be part of a night full of authentic expression and good vibes.</p>
